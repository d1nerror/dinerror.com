Forbidden Memories fusion calculator Web app

Role:
UI/UX designer and builder
Scope:
UX research, information architecture,UI design, responsive web app
Type:
Self‑initiated, open source

Problem

Hundreds of combinations.No guide.

Yu‑Gi‑Oh! Forbidden Memories came out on the PS1 in 2002 with no guide to how cards fuse.It plays by its own rules, not the card game’s, so every move was a guess.With 722 cards and hundreds of combinations, trial and error was hopeless,and the frustration started on day one.

Solution

Three views.One question each.

Hand: type up to five cardsand see every fusion you can make, strongest first.

Lookup: pick any of the 722 cardsto see what it fuses with, what makes it,and its Guardian Stars.

Deck: see how well your deck fuses,with dead cards, Guardian Star gapsand which card to add next.

It works on phone and desktop,saves your deck automatically,and every change has an Undo instead of a confirm.

Identity

Gold frames.Old lettering.

The look borrows from the old card borders: gold double frames, a dark gold palette and Cinzel titles. Body text uses the device’s own font, so it stays fast and easy to read.

Result

Open it when you’re stuck.

A free app that works offline,on a phone next to the console.Open source under GPL‑3.0,with card data from the YGO FM Database.

A fan project, not affiliated with Konami.
